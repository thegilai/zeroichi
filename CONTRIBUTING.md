# Helping with Zero Ichi

Thanks for being here!

## Say hello or share your photos

Took some photos with Zero Ichi? Built your own camera? Have a question or an
idea? Come and chat in [Discussions](../../discussions).

## Something not working?

Open a [new issue](../../issues/new/choose) and tell us what happened.

It really helps if you also add these two files. You'll find them on the
camera's SD card, in the `zeroichi` folder:

- `zeroichi-log.txt`
- `boot-time.txt`

They don't contain anything personal.

## Want to change the code?

You're welcome to! Make your change and send a pull request explaining what
it does. Please keep Zero Ichi simple and offline: no Wi-Fi, no Bluetooth, no
accounts.

## Be kind

This is a small project made for fun. Please be friendly to everyone.
